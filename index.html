About me

I'm a scientist and engineer from Santiago, Chile, with a PhD in Fluid Dynamics and Climatology and over 10 years of experience working with data.

My work bridges research and real-world industrial applications by using machine learning, automation and optimization algorithms, and time series prediction to turn complex processes into better operational decisions. I enjoy turning complex problems into simple, beautiful, automated plots.

As a data scientist my approach is to detect patterns and anomalies, based on the physical phenomena, extracting the hidden information within data to forecast trends with high accuracy.

By combining my background in astronomy, fluid dynamics, and climatology with advanced data analytics and cloud tools, my approach is to understand data behavior by making it stop being a black box and become a practical tool to drive innovation and understand the world.
